# Atlantic Council 2026 Cyber 9/12 Strategy Challenge Austin, Texas

## Overview
* **Event:** Atlantic Council 2026 Cyber 9/12 Strategy Challenge (Austin, Texas).
* **Result:** **1st place in “Presentation Skills & Policy Proposals,” 13th place overall, certificate award.**
* **Team:** Lewis University, Order of the Purple Flamingo
* **Program:** [Atlantic Council Cyber Statecraft Initiative – Cyber 9/12 Strategy Challenge](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/programs/cyber-statecraft-initiative/cyber-912/)
* **Info:** 25 Competitive Teams across the United States and internationally. Including Stanford and University of Illinois.
* **Role:** Policy creation, intelligence reports analysis, 2 page situation briefs, 2 page written briefs, 1 page decision documents, oral presentations, and questioning to and from judges from the White House, Department of Defense, Department of Justice, security architects at Google, Microsoft, and CISO's from Austin, TX in a 16 hour turnaround.

## Operational Responsibilities & Contributions
* **Crisis Response & Policy Analysis:**

Analyzed intelligence reports under a ~16-hour turnaround to formulate actionable, national-level cyber policy recommendations addressing an evolving international cyber crisis involving APT "PESTIS".
* **Strategic Stabilization & Risk Containment:**

Engineered a phased policy response plan covering immediate economic risk containment, infrastructure stabilization, TTP-based eradication, and cloud provider coordination.
* **Intelligence & Legal Preservation:**

Authored recommendations for limited IoC declassification by the ODNI and DOJ, balancing public-private sector collaboration with the preservation of adversary C2 infrastructure.
* **Executive & Stakeholder Communication:**

Delivered high-stakes oral briefings and Q&A sessions to expert judges with senior national security, government, and industry experience (White House, NSC, NSA, FBI, DOD, DOJ, U.S. Navy, Microsoft, Google, City of Austin).
